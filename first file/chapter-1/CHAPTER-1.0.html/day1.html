The Elfs Ant

Once upon a time, a tiny elf had a pet ant that he loved very much. He wanted his ant to grow big and strong, so he fed it all the best food he could find. The ant was quite a "gobbler" and ate everything in sight!As time passed, the ant kept eating and growing.
It grew so much that it eventually couldn't even fit inside the elf's room! Reluctantly, the elf had to give his pet to the local zoo so it would have enough space. The ant became quite a celebrity at the zoo because of its massive size. Over time, people began calling the "elf's ant" the "elephant." Today, when you visit the zoo, you might see an elephant and never know the funny story of how it got its name!
